Our Control System

The ultimate goal of this project is to control the spin of the rocket on the way up. Rockets can be controlled by various means such as cold gas thrusters, thrust vectoring, and what we chose for our project, fin actuation.

Pros of Fin Actuation

Lightweight

Less weight means the rocket is more efficient

Inexpensive

With an already tight budget, saving money is always helpful

Widely Used

With plenty of other work and documentation to refer to we can more easily overcome challenges

Simple

Easy to understand on an intuitive level

Cons of Fin Actuation

Can Change Multiple Axes

It can be easy to steer the rocket in an undesirable direction

Variable with Rocket Velocity

As the rocket slows the amount of steering from the fins lessens

Difficult to Ground Test

Since the rocket can reach speeds of up to 500 mph and the fins depend on wind speed to steer, it will be especially difficult to test this prior to the final launch
